LinkX Ideas

Technology Still Comes First.

The AGI inflection point has not yet arrived. The core task is twofold: keep pushing the intelligence ceiling, and learn how to reliably unlock capabilities that models already possess in real-world settings.

Back to Excerpts

Scaling Remains the Most Efficient Way to Raise the Ceiling

Larger datasets, including synthetic and distilled data, larger parameter counts, and more compute remain necessary. Even after two years of multimodal and end-to-end speech exploration, text still appears to be the core substrate for pushing the intelligence ceiling.

Alignment, Reasoning, and Activation Are the Unlocks

  • Reinforcement learning is gradually replacing SFT and chain-of-thought tuning as the dominant alignment path. Mid-training and post-training should make fast alignment possible across more scenarios.
  • Cross-environment generalization remains a hard problem for agents. The most direct path is to create more environment data and run reinforcement learning across different environments.
  • The next breakthrough is likely to involve memory, online learning, and self-evaluation. Context, RAG, and parameters roughly map to short-, medium-, and long-term memory; self-evaluation may become the basis for a new scaling paradigm.

Application-Layer Strategy

  • Application-layer products need to stay thin enough to adapt as model capabilities evolve quickly.
  • Domain-specific models may eventually be absorbed by stronger foundation models, but they still have a real competitive window today.
  • Products should be organized around actual work types, not SaaS-era software categories.
Scaling
Still the most efficient path for improving model capability; text remains the core medium.
Memory
A three-layer memory architecture: context for short-term memory, RAG for medium-term memory, and parameters for long-term memory.
Self-Evaluation
Continual, real-time, and online learning may become a new scaling paradigm.

What to Watch

  • Whether mid-training and post-training can rapidly align models to long-tail real-world scenarios.
  • Whether continual, online, and real-time learning become a new scaling paradigm.
  • Whether domain models become durable companies or are ultimately absorbed by stronger general-purpose foundation models.
Onepage · Agent OS

From Reactive Software to Agentic Systems.

OpenClaw is not just a product story. It is a visible sign of a broader shift: software is moving from something users operate to something that can act on their behalf.

Back to Excerpts

Adoption Signals

OpenClaw surpassed 250K GitHub stars in 60 days, overtaking React (243K) and the Linux kernel (218K) as one of the fastest-growing software projects in GitHub history. ClawHub accumulated 26K skills, and the Discord community reached 120K members.

China is moving faster on application adoption for the first time: domestic model costs are 60-80% lower, and multiple local governments have launched support programs, including up to ¥10M in Hefei and a roughly ¥3B industry fund in Wuxi.

Key Bottlenecks

  • Token Cost: Agents consume 100-1000× more tokens than traditional chat. Each loop can reload conversation history, the system prompt, and tool definitions, creating real cost risk, including cases of losing $120 overnight to retry loops.
  • Robustness: EvoClaw testing shows that the most advanced agents achieve only a 38% average success rate on long-sequence tasks, falling to 13.37% in the most complex scenarios.
  • Security: The ecosystem has already seen 258 historical vulnerabilities, with malicious-code rates in ClawHub plugins estimated at 10.8%-26%.

Paradigm Shift

  • The competitive unit shifts from the model alone to the system stack: model, runtime, memory, toolchain, evaluation, observability, and human interface.
  • The economic unit shifts from "parameter scale" to "per-token output efficiency" — tokens per dollar, tokens per watt, total task completion cost.
  • Whoever controls the runtime may also control training, because execution traces, feedback, and RL loops are generated inside the runtime.

Industry Layering View

  • Model / Platform Layer (OpenAI / Anthropic): Developer OS, controlling model + Agent OS + interface. Anthropic is building across MCP, Claude Code, economic indices, and agent autonomy, while OpenAI is integrating Computer Use, agents, tools, and containers. The fight is over default foundations and default protocols.
  • Middle Layer: The core value is making agents truly usable by designing the runtime environment around them. This includes orchestration, workflows, memory, evaluation, safety, and observability around loops and token cost. One likely form is TaaS, as in Mechanize or Beam AI-Runtime.
  • Application layer: New product forms may emerge as human-in-the-loop tools evolve into human-on-the-loop control panels. Agent value will increasingly depend on safe execution environments, composable skills and plugins, and workflow-native interfaces.

Onepage · Self-Evolution

From Scaling Models to Scaling Experience.

AI self-evolution reframes scaling: from scaling parameters, data, and compute to scaling experience, feedback, experiments, and discovery loops. L3 is the current verifiable inflection point; L4 is the watershed.

Back to Excerpts

Levels of Self-Evolution

  • L0: Point tool → L1: Single-task self-correction → L2: Cross-task experience accumulation
  • L3: Automated research loop (hypothesis → experiment → feedback → revised hypothesis) → current verifiable inflection point
  • L4: Capability feedback loop (experience feeds into next-gen model) → the true watershed
  • L5: Open-ended recursive self-improvement → highly uncertain

Feedback Hierarchy (V5→V0)

The strongest verification comes from code and math (immediate, objective, reproducible); the biggest challenge is at V0 open-ended innovation (highest risk of illusory progress). Code tests, math proofs, simulations, and lab experiments are the most investable early environments.

Representative Funding Signals

  • Recursive: $650M funding, $4.65B valuation
  • Ineffable: $1.1B funding, $5.1B valuation
  • Periodic Labs: ~$300M seed
  • Isomorphic Labs: $2.1B funding in 2026

Frontier Developments

  • OpenAI model disproves discrete geometry conjecture (L2-L3 level)
  • AlphaEvolve achieves "generate → evaluate → iterate" loop (L3)
  • Sakana AI Scientist-v2 paper passes ICLR blind review (L3 exploring toward L4)

Deep Research · Video Generation

Physical Realism vs. Controllability: The Paths Are Diverging.

Both models are first-tier, but their technical paths are diverging. Vidu leans toward physical consistency and world-model exploration; Seedance leans toward controllable production workflows and real-time interactive video.

Back to Excerpts

Architectural Differences

  • Vidu Q3: U-ViT (All-as-words unified sequence) + long skip connections maintaining spatiotemporal consistency. TurboDiffusion compresses single RTX 5090 1080p generation from 900s to 8s.
  • Seedance 2.0: MMDiT dual-branch + spatiotemporal decoupling + MoE sparsification + inference-stage Refiner Model. Up to 12 inputs of multimodal material combinations.

Key Metrics

  • Vidu supports 1–16s 1080p; Seedance supports 4–15s, up to 2K.
  • Pricing: Vidu standard ~¥1.92/s; Seedance basic ~¥0.3/s. ByteDance has significant pricing advantage.
  • Shengshu achieved 10×+ growth in users and revenue in 2025, covering 90%+ of film & TV content companies (Sony, Tencent Animation, Yuewen, etc.).

Where the Paths Diverge

  • Vidu needs to remove the crutch: Move beyond long skip-connection scaffolding, shift to native spatiotemporal consistency modeling, moving toward cinematic-grade 3D video.
  • Seedance needs to get leaner: Flatten architecture to reduce communication overhead, support real-time interaction and move toward interactive 3D interfaces for AR and VR.

Commercialization Assessment

Seedance is better suited for creators and professional users who need predictability, storyboard control, and integration with existing short-video workflows. Its ByteDance ecosystem, including Jimeng, CapCut, and Douyin, gives it strong consumer reach. Vidu may be more differentiated in premium creative use cases, overseas enterprise expansion, and scenarios that require physical realism, native visual dynamics, or future adjacency to world models. Its coverage of 90%+ of film and TV content companies, including Sony, Tencent Animation, and Yuewen, gives it a clearer enterprise wedge.

Deep Research · Capital Landscape

Capital Is Moving Earlier, While Scale Concentrates.

China's 2025 private market is increasingly led by state-backed and industrial capital, while USD capital plays a more supplementary role. AI funding is concentrated in embodied AI, autonomous driving, and general-purpose models for enterprise scenarios.

Back to Excerpts

China's Private Market

In 2025, China recorded 9,058 private-market deals, up 28% year over year. Early-stage deals accounted for 66.8%, a five-year high, but thousands of early-stage companies received only about 25% of market capital. Strategic investments represented 11.76% of deals but absorbed ¥359.1B.

AI Funding Flows

  • In AI, 930 companies raised a total of ¥107.07B.
  • Embodied AI leads with 194 companies and ¥33.77B in funding, growing nearly 2× YoY (305+ deals).
  • Enterprise-oriented projects account for 62.17% of the top 10 tracks.
  • Eight major technology groups made 62 investments during the year, led by Baidu Ventures with 13.

Global Comparison

  • Global 2025 AI funding reached $225.8B (nearly 2× of 2024).
  • LLM developers captured 41% of all AI funding ($93.1B); OpenAI + Anthropic + xAI combined $86.3B.
  • In the US, more than 40% of capital went to large-model platforms. In China, capital concentrated in robotics, autonomous driving, and AI plus manufacturing, with almost no $1B+ AI mega-rounds.
